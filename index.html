About

The meaning of "Dingified"

Dingi Couture turns an everyday material — pipe cleaners — into flowers, characters, accessories, décor, and anything else worth imagining. "Dingified" means taking an ordinary idea and giving it a personal, handmade twist. It's not about one product or one trend. It's about seeing what happens when you point a pair of hands and a coil of fuzzy wire at literally anything.
